    About you

    Exactly as it should appear on your EPMS form.

    Your supervisor (appraiser)

    The officer who appraises you directly, usually your Assistant Director of Nursing.

    Counter-signing officer

    The senior officer who counter-signs your appraisal, usually your Deputy Director of Nursing.

    Your tasks (KRAs) for the year

    Tick the Key Result Areas from the hospital's performance contract that you worked on. Most nurses pick 2 to 6. Search by a word (e.g. "eye", "theatre", "documentation") or by your unit's code. If yours is not listed, add it at the bottom.

    Pick at least one task before you continue.

    Monthly results

    For each month, choose two of your tasks and enter each one's Output Status: your score for that task in that month. Some tasks are percentages (0 to 100); others are numbers, such as how many screenings or trainings you held. Under each box you'll see your contract target and the rating scale. Falling short of the target is not wrong: enter your true figure and your supervisor rates it on that scale. Leave a month blank if you do not have its figures yet.

    Fill at least one month: choose a task and enter its Output Status. You can add the other months later with your edit link.

    A few words about your year

    All optional. Short notes are fine; they help fill the comment boxes on your reviews.
